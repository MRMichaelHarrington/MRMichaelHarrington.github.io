Portfolio

Ideas built to move from research into practice.

Michael Harrington's portfolio connects institutional discipline, artificial intelligence, investor learning and a long-term commitment to purposeful impact.

A connected body of work

One philosophy, expressed through different institutions.

Each initiative addresses a different part of the investing journey: professional portfolio leadership, research technology, intelligent decision support, practical education and the broader responsibility that can follow financial success.

Investment leadership

Peak Hedge Strategies

Peak Hedge Strategies represents the institutional chapter of Michael Harrington's profile. As a stated co-founder, he brought quantitative research, portfolio construction and risk governance into a structure designed to make decisions repeatable across changing market conditions. The work emphasized that an investment firm needs more than a model: it needs clear responsibility, operational discipline and a process for responding when correlations, liquidity or market behavior move outside expected ranges.

Artificial intelligence

VeritasMind AI

VeritasMind AI was conceived around a direct question: how can computation improve the quality of financial judgment without obscuring the reasoning behind a decision? The initiative brings artificial intelligence and quantitative research into the same environment, with emphasis on information quality, model interpretation and human oversight. Its place in the portfolio is foundational, providing the research perspective from which more specialized decision systems can develop.

Decision intelligence

QuantCore 5.0

QuantCore 5.0 is presented as the fifth stage of an evolving AI trading framework. It brings deep learning, reinforcement learning, on-chain data, dark-pool observation, decentralized-finance strategies and traditional asset allocation into a common research architecture. Michael Harrington describes its role as an intelligent financial assistant: a system that organizes information, supports scenario evaluation and helps users develop a more deliberate relationship with risk.

Investor education

VMA Community

VMA Community extends the technology philosophy into an educational setting. Free learning resources, guided strategy work and practical market discussion are designed to help participants move from receiving conclusions toward understanding how a conclusion is formed. Michael Harrington's stated ambition is to make institutional habits—questioning evidence, defining risk and reviewing outcomes—more accessible to people building their own investment knowledge.

Public mission

Education and mentorship

Teaching is not treated as an afterthought to investment work. It is the method through which accumulated experience becomes useful to another generation. Michael Harrington's educational direction favors applied learning: students form hypotheses, observe markets, discuss trade-offs and learn to separate a repeatable process from a single favorable outcome. The aim is confidence grounded in understanding rather than certainty built on a signal.

Long-term impact

Philanthropy and social responsibility

Michael Harrington's stated philanthropic goals focus on education and environmental protection. The principle is that financial freedom creates the capacity to choose where time, knowledge and capital can have lasting value. By connecting an investor community with charitable participation, this part of the portfolio seeks to turn private achievement into broader contribution while keeping purpose connected to practical action.

The operating idea

Technology should deepen understanding.

The thread connecting these projects is the belief that financial technology reaches its highest value when it improves the user's capacity to reason. Speed matters. Data coverage matters. Yet neither solves the problem of judgment on its own.

Michael Harrington's portfolio therefore treats explanation, education and risk framing as functional parts of a system. A user should be able to understand why information matters, what conditions could change its meaning and how a decision fits within a broader plan.

Beyond markets

Success creates room for responsibility.

Investment achievement becomes more meaningful when it supports family, learning, mentorship and public contribution. Michael Harrington's later-career vision gives these priorities the same deliberate attention once reserved for portfolio construction.

The result is a broader definition of legacy: systems that continue to teach, communities that share practical knowledge and philanthropic efforts that translate personal freedom into opportunity for others.
