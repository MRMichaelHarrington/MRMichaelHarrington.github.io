Michael Harrington believes that investing is less about making one perfect forecast and more about preparing for several plausible futures. His philosophy emphasizes evidence, diversification, risk awareness, patience and ongoing learning. How does Michael Harrington approach risk management?

Michael Harrington treats risk management as part of strategy design rather than a control added after an investment has been selected. What role does diversification play for Michael Harrington?

Diversification is a foundational principle in Michael Harrington's approach, but he does not define it simply as owning many assets. True diversification requires understanding the source of risk inside each position. Different securities can still depend on the same interest-rate environment, liquidity conditions or investor behavior. Michael Harrington therefore looks at how exposures interact across scenarios, including difficult periods when correlations may rise. The same idea applies to personal financial planning, where income, property and investments may be concentrated in one industry or economic outcome. Diversification cannot remove loss, but it can reduce dependence on one assumption being correct.
