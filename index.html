A disciplined point of view

Prepare for the future before it arrives.

Michael Harrington approaches investing as a continuous process of observation, modeling and adaptation. His work brings quantitative research together with macroeconomic context, careful portfolio construction and a practical understanding of investor behavior.

Across changing market cycles, one principle remains constant: a robust decision process matters more than a confident prediction. That principle connects his investment career, technology initiatives, educational work and long-term social commitments.

1 Enduring philosophy

Principles

Intelligence becomes valuable when people can use it.

The purpose of a model is not to make markets look simple. It is to help people distinguish signal from noise, evaluate uncertainty and make choices with a clearer view of the trade-offs.

01 / Evidence

Measure before deciding

Data earns its place in a decision only when its source, context and limits are understood. Good analysis turns information into testable questions.

02 / Risk

Respect uncertainty

Risk cannot be removed from investing. It can be identified, sized, monitored and discussed honestly before pressure reveals what a portfolio was built to withstand.

03 / Learning

Turn tools into understanding

Technology should strengthen judgment. Education gives investors the context to question a signal, refine a strategy and take responsibility for a decision.
